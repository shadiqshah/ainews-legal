Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

AI News Studio is a personal application used only by its owner. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data about any other person.

When the owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit, the application receives an access token for the owner's own TikTok account (scopes: user.info.basic, video.upload). The token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own account as drafts. No data is sent to any third party. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings, Security, Manage app permissions.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/shadiqshah/ainews-legal.
